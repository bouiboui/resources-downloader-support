# Resources Downloader Pro refund policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Resources Downloader Pro is sold as a one-time license through Lemon Squeezy.
You may request a refund within 14 calendar days of purchase if the paid
features do not work for your legitimate use case or the purchase was made by
mistake.

Reply to the private support contact in your Lemon Squeezy receipt or order
page, or email [dev@cod3.net](mailto:dev@cod3.net). Include the order number and
a short description of the problem. Do not post order details or license keys in
a public GitHub issue.

For an approved refund, the associated Pro license is disabled as a separate
operational step. The extension removes Pro at its next online validation; a
device that remains offline can retain cached access only until the 30-day grace
period expires. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and may take
additional banking time to appear. This policy does not limit any mandatory
consumer rights that apply in your location.

Requests involving fraud, abuse, or a materially consumed digital service may
require additional review by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.
